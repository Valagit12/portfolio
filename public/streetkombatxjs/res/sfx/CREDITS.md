Each file is named after the sound it replaces in `audio/sfx.js` (the
synthesized fallback). Dom's hits and the dumbbell clang also keep their
synthesized iron ring, layered on top at play time (`renderRing` in sfx.js). The recordings were trimmed, layered and normalized,
then encoded as mono 96 kbps MP3.
